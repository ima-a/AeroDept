**Separate teacher and matched student experiment.** Use the same expanded train manifest and unchanged two-path validation for all arms. Use new output/config names; do not resume, fine-tune in place, replace or relabel original epoch-4 weights or the historical custom-CNN pilot as a new baseline.

Teacher proposal: TorchVision ResNet-50 encoder with an explicitly pinned ImageNet weight enum (propose IMAGENET1K_V2, record cached hash/source); discard classifier/global pooling and attach a learned multiscale skip decoder, projecting encoder features to laptop-sized channels and upsampling to 192×320 with a one-channel softplus + 0.1 m metric-range head. ImageNet classification weights alone are not a depth teacher. Train the decoder and encoder on expanded TRAIN labels; select its checkpoint only on original VAL AbsRel. [TorchVision's ResNet-50 documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.resnet50.html) documents the encoder/weight choices; keep this project's full-frame depth preprocessing instead of applying classification centre-crop transforms.

Student: keep the existing MobileNetV3SmallDepth architecture/decoder and 1,027,489-parameter contract. Per the updated experiment instruction, independently initialize both the supervised-only expanded-data arm and the distilled arm from the exact published epoch-4 state dict, including BatchNorm buffers (SHA-256 `b40c37db3507cd22d39fcfeba6e23067fb619aee4e3ca61735a7698515006aa6`). Start fresh optimizer, scheduler, scaler, history and counters; reset training/data RNG identically after model construction. This initialization is separate from resume. The teacher alone uses ImageNet initialization. Pair train order, augmentations, initial tensors, physical/effective batch, optimizer, LR schedule/horizon, total epochs and validation selection; the only arm difference is the declared distillation term and teacher computation. Record any resource-driven differences; do not claim a clean distillation ablation if they diverge.

Initial contract remains RGB [0,1], full-frame bilinear align_corners=False resize to 192×320, ImageNet normalization once inside each model, no crop, training paired horizontal flip only. MidAir targets remain Euclidean camera range in metres, uint16 PNG reinterpreted float16 bits, valid finite inclusive [0.1,1000] m before nearest resizing of target/mask; near-field GT <10 m. Positive outputs have floor 0.1 m and no upper clamp or fitted scale. Keep supervised L1/log/gradient weights 1/0.2/0.1, near loss 0. Keep pooled valid-pixel AbsRel/RMSE/delta1/2/3 and near AbsRel/RMSE; report per-path and pooled metrics, counts and floor saturation. Use FP32 validation audit at a common batch/precision; keep the original historical AMP checkpoint-selection label unchanged.

Proposed new-run settings start from the recorded AdamW 3e-4, weight decay 1e-4, gradient clip 1, AMP on CUDA, fixed 50-epoch cosine horizon and eta_min 3e-6; no scheduler search. Students target effective batch 4. Teacher microbatch and decoder widths require a later measured memory check; gradient accumulation may be needed and must be recorded. If small batches make BatchNorm unstable, explicitly freeze encoder running statistics with a recorded policy and keep student arms matched. Do not assume the original 16.5-hour student duration or tiny-model allocator peak applies to a ResNet teacher or 2.5× larger training set. No full-run duration is authorized by this planning record.

Train the matched supervised student and teacher first. Before any distillation optimization, require a teacher validation gate: finite metric-range predictions and correct pixel counts; teacher pooled FP32 AbsRel strictly below the expanded supervised student; and on EACH of the two validation paths, teacher overall AbsRel/RMSE and near AbsRel/RMSE no worse than that student. Report near/far errors, saturation and paired validation images. This deliberately conservative proposed gate must be frozen in the executable new protocol before training. If it fails, mark the teacher unsuitable and stop before distillation; no test evaluation or test-informed adjustment. The old epoch-4 validation results may provide historical context but are not the matched expanded-data control.

Initial distillation proposal: output-only, frozen selected teacher in eval/no_grad, using the identical augmented RGB tensor supplied to the student. Total loss = existing supervised loss + 0.2 × masked mean |log(student range) − log(detached teacher range)| on valid TRAIN GT pixels with finite positive teacher outputs. The 0.2 coefficient is an untested planning choice, to freeze before training; no feature adapters or test-driven weight search. Supervised-only arm uses coefficient 0. No scale alignment or classification KL temperature. Compute/check teacher targets in FP32 where needed. No gradients or BatchNorm updates may reach the teacher. Validation selects student best by minimum original-validation AbsRel (propose common FP32 selection for the new arms), with no KD term deciding the checkpoint. Final evaluation waits for a new frozen single-exposure protocol containing all arms and metrics.

Online frozen-teacher predictions are the initial simple design; benchmark memory with the student on the 4 GB laptop before accepting it. An offline train-only FP32 output cache would require exactly 10,837,524,480 bytes for 44,098 × 192 × 320 predictions, before indexing/metadata/filesystem overhead; this is a calculated tensor size, not a measured dataset size. Flipped RGB requires matching teacher inference or separately keyed flipped targets; a flipped cached unflipped prediction is not guaranteed equivalent. Never generate teacher caches for new test during preparation. Select a feasible strategy in a later bounded implementation check, not a full run now.
